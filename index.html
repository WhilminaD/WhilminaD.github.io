Rates

UGC is priced per video and sponsored posts per placement — no follower-count maths, just a clear quote up front. Bundles for multi-video campaigns are available, and Toronto-based brands can book on-location filming around the city. Email me your brief and timeline and I'll reply with concepts and a quote.
